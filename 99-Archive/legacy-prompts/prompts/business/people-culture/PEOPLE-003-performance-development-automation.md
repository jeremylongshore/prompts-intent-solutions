## LEGAL CONSIDERATIONS

**IMPORTANT**: This performance management system must comply with employment laws:

### Documentation Requirements
- **Performance records**: Maintain objective, factual documentation
- **Development plans**: Keep records of commitments and progress
- **Improvement plans**: Document performance issues and interventions
- **Decision rationale**: Record basis for performance-related decisions

### Bias Prevention and Fairness
- **Consistent application**: Use same standards across all employees
- **Objective criteria**: Base evaluations on job-related competencies
- **Training**: Educate managers on unconscious bias and fair evaluation
- **Review process**: Implement calibration to ensure consistency

### Legal Compliance Areas
- **ADA accommodation**: Adjust performance standards for disabilities
- **FMLA integration**: Account for protected leave in evaluations
- **Protected class considerations**: Ensure no discriminatory impact
- **At-will employment**: Include appropriate disclaimers in documentation

## TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE

### Common Implementation Challenges

**Challenge**: "Managers avoid difficult performance conversations"
**Solution**:
- Provide conversation scripts and frameworks
- Offer HR support for challenging discussions
- Train on conflict resolution and empathetic communication
- Create safe practice environments before real conversations

**Challenge**: "Employees don't trust the review process"
**Solution**:
- Ensure transparency in evaluation criteria
- Provide examples of fair, consistent application
- Allow employee input and appeal processes
- Demonstrate action taken based on feedback

**Challenge**: "Goal setting becomes meaningless busy work"
**Solution**:
- Connect goals directly to business outcomes
- Provide templates for meaningful, achievable goals
- Track and celebrate goal achievement publicly
- Link goals to career advancement opportunities

**Challenge**: "360-degree feedback creates conflict"
**Solution**:
- Train all participants on constructive feedback
- Ensure anonymity and aggregate reporting
- Focus feedback on behaviors, not personality
- Provide coaching on receiving and acting on feedback
