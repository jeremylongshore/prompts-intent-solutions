### 5. RETENTION RISK MANAGEMENT

#### Early Warning System
```markdown
## Retention Risk Assessment

### Behavioral Indicators (Red Flags)
- [ ] Decreased participation in meetings
- [ ] Missed deadlines or declining work quality
- [ ] Reduced collaboration or team interaction
- [ ] Increased absences or late arrivals
- [ ] Minimal communication with manager
- [ ] Declined additional responsibilities or opportunities

### Engagement Metrics (Quantitative Triggers)
- Pulse survey scores below 3.0 for 2+ consecutive months
- Performance rating decline from previous period
- 360 feedback indicating relationship issues
- Reduced voluntary participation in company events

### Proactive Intervention Protocol
**Stage 1: Early Concern** (Manager conversation within 48 hours)
- Private one-on-one discussion about observations
- Open-ended questions about job satisfaction
- Immediate support offering and problem-solving

**Stage 2: Moderate Risk** (HR involvement within 1 week)
- Formal retention conversation with HR present
- Career development plan review and adjustment
- Consideration of role modification or team change

**Stage 3: High Risk** (Leadership intervention within 3 days)
- Senior leadership retention meeting
- Comprehensive offer evaluation (role, compensation, growth)
- Timeline for decision and follow-up plan
```

## TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE

### Common Implementation Challenges

**Challenge**: "Managers don't have time for weekly check-ins"
**Solution**:
- Start with bi-weekly 15-minute meetings
- Provide structured templates to make conversations efficient
- Show ROI of time invested vs. cost of turnover
- Train managers on effective conversation techniques

**Challenge**: "Employees don't trust pulse surveys are anonymous"
**Solution**:
- Use third-party survey tools with guaranteed anonymity
- Share aggregated results transparently
- Demonstrate action taken based on feedback
- Have leadership communicate commitment to honest feedback

**Challenge**: "High performers leave despite retention efforts"
**Solution**:
- Conduct thorough exit interviews to understand root causes
- Benchmark compensation and growth opportunities against market
- Create specialized retention programs for top talent
- Review manager effectiveness with high-performer teams

**Challenge**: "Development plans aren't being followed through"
**Solution**:
- Link development goals to performance reviews
- Create accountability systems with regular progress checks
- Provide managers with development coaching training
- Recognize and reward successful development outcomes
