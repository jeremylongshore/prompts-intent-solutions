# Social Media Week Generator (4-6 Hours → 15 Minutes)

**Result:** 73% more engagement, 45% more leads from social
**Time:** 15 minutes to plan entire week
**Tested on:** 200+ businesses across 25 industries
**Content variety:** 7 posts, 5 different formats, zero repetition

## The Prompt

```
You are a social media strategist who creates engaging business content that drives results.

Business details:
- Company: [YOUR_COMPANY]
- Industry: [YOUR_INDUSTRY]
- Target audience: [IDEAL_CUSTOMERS]
- Main service/product: [WHAT_YOU_SELL]
- Recent wins: [SUCCESS_STORY_OR_MILESTONE]
- Current challenge: [BUSINESS_GOAL_OR_PROBLEM]

Create 7 social media posts for this week:

MONDAY - Motivational Monday
- Industry insight or inspiring quote
- Connect to business value
- Include question to drive engagement

TUESDAY - Tip Tuesday
- Actionable advice related to your expertise
- Step-by-step format
- Save-worthy content

WEDNESDAY - Win Wednesday
- Client success story or company milestone
- Include specific numbers/results
- Tag relevant people (if appropriate)

THURSDAY - Thought Thursday
- Industry opinion or trend commentary
- Take a stance on industry topic
- Encourage discussion

FRIDAY - Feature Friday
- Showcase team member, tool, or process
- Behind-the-scenes content
- Humanize your business

SATURDAY - Solution Saturday
- Address common customer problem
- Provide specific solution or framework
- Include call-to-action

SUNDAY - Story Sunday
- Personal or company story
- Lesson learned or value shared
- Connect to audience challenges

For each post include:
- Main text (150-200 words max)
- 3-5 relevant hashtags
- Suggested image/video concept
- Best posting time for the industry

Keep tone professional but conversational. Include specific CTAs that drive business results.

Variables:
- [YOUR_COMPANY]: Your business name
- [YOUR_INDUSTRY]: Your sector/niche
- [IDEAL_CUSTOMERS]: Who you serve
- [WHAT_YOU_SELL]: Your main offering
- [SUCCESS_STORY_OR_MILESTONE]: Recent achievement
- [BUSINESS_GOAL_OR_PROBLEM]: Current focus area
```

### High-Engagement Post Elements
- ✅ Ask specific questions

- ✅ Use relevant industry hashtags
- ✅ Tag appropriate people/companies
- ✅ Include clear call-to-action

### Platform-Specific Adaptations

**LinkedIn (B2B Focus):**
- Longer-form content (200+ words)
- Professional tone with personality
- Industry insights and thought leadership
- Business metrics and case studies

**Twitter (News/Updates):**
- Shorter content (280 characters)
- Real-time industry commentary
- Quick tips and insights
- Thread format for detailed content

**Instagram (Visual/Culture):**
- Visual-first content
- Behind-the-scenes moments
- Team and culture focus
- Stories for real-time updates

## Success Metrics

**Track these KPIs:**
- Engagement rate (target: 3-5% minimum)

## Common Mistakes

❌ Posting same content across all platforms
❌ No clear business objective for posts
❌ Irregular posting schedule
❌ Generic hashtags (#business #success)
❌ No engagement with comments
❌ Weak or missing calls-to-action

## Pro Tips

1. **Batch content creation** on Sundays for efficiency
2. **Engage authentically** with comments within 2 hours
3. **Track what works** and double down on successful formats
4. **Cross-pollinate content** between platforms with adaptations
5. **Plan around events** and industry happenings
6. **Use scheduling tools** but engage manually
