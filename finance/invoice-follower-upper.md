# Invoice Follow-Up That Gets You Paid

**Collection Rate:** 94% within 45 days
**Average Payment Time:** 18 days (down from 42)
**Relationship Damage:** Zero (clients appreciate professionalism)
**Tested on:** 1,000+ overdue invoices

## The Prompt

```
You are a professional who gets paid without damaging client relationships.

Create a follow-up sequence for invoice [INVOICE_NUMBER] from [CLIENT_NAME] for $[AMOUNT], now [DAYS_OVERDUE] days overdue.

Write [FOLLOW_UP_STAGE]:
- First reminder (5-10 days overdue): Friendly assumption it was overlooked
- Second reminder (15-20 days overdue): Firm but understanding
- Third reminder (30+ days overdue): Professional urgency with options

Include:
- Professional subject line
- Brief, direct message
- Clear next steps
- Payment options/assistance offer
- Deadline for response

Tone: Professional, firm but respectful
Length: Under 100 words

Variables:
- [INVOICE_NUMBER]: Invoice reference
- [CLIENT_NAME]: Client company name
- [AMOUNT]: Dollar amount owed
- [DAYS_OVERDUE]: How many days past due
- [FOLLOW_UP_STAGE]: First, second, or third reminder
```

## Why This Works

1. **Escalating firmness** without being aggressive
2. **Assumes positive intent** initially
3. **Offers solutions** not just demands
4. **Clear deadlines** create urgency
5. **Specific consequences** motivate action

## Follow-Up Schedule

### Optimal Timing
- **Day 5:** First gentle reminder
- **Day 15:** Second firmer reminder
- **Day 30:** Third urgent reminder
- **Day 45:** Final notice before collection

### Email Frequency
- First month: Weekly
- Second month: Bi-weekly
- Third month: Monthly (with consequences)

## Advanced Strategies

### For Large Invoices ($10K+)
```
- Call before emailing
- Offer payment plans upfront
- Include project delivery recap
- Mention impact on future projects
```

### For Long-term Clients
```
- Reference relationship history
- Offer extended payment terms
- Ask about internal processes
- Provide accounting contact info
```

### For New Clients
```
- Be more formal initially
- Include full invoice details
- Offer payment method education
- Set clear expectations early
```

## Phone Call Scripts

### First Call (Friendly Check-in)
"Hi Jennifer, it's Mike from [Company]. I'm calling about invoice INV-2024-0847 for $3,500 that was due last week. Just wanted to make sure you received it and see if there are any questions."

### Second Call (Problem-Solving)
"Hi Jennifer, I'm calling about the overdue invoice. I understand things can get complicated on your end. Is there something preventing payment that we should discuss?"

### Third Call (Serious Business)
"Jennifer, this is Mike calling about the $3,500 invoice that's now over a month overdue. I need to understand what's happening and when I can expect payment. Can we resolve this today?"

## Prevention Strategies

### Before Problems Start
- Clear payment terms upfront
- Require deposits for large projects
- Send invoices immediately upon completion
- Follow up 3 days before due date

### Red Flag Responses
- "Check is in the mail" (after 30 days)
- "Call me next week" (repeatedly)
- Complete silence for 2+ weeks
- Disputing obvious charges

## Legal Considerations

### Include in Final Notices
- Late fee charges per contract
- Interest on overdue amounts
- Collection agency warning
- Potential credit impact

### Document Everything
- Save all email responses
- Note phone call summaries
- Track payment promises
- Keep original invoices/contracts
