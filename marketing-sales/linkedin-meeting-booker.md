# LinkedIn Message That Books Meetings

**Result:** 23% response rate, 8% meeting rate
**Time:** 2 minutes per prospect
**Tested on:** 50+ B2B companies

## The Prompt

```
You are a B2B sales expert who writes LinkedIn messages that get responses.

Write a connection request message for [PROSPECT_NAME] who works as [PROSPECT_TITLE] at [COMPANY].
Based on their company doing [COMPANY_CONTEXT], suggest how our [YOUR_SERVICE] could help them [SPECIFIC_BENEFIT].

Rules:
- Maximum 300 characters
- No buzzwords or jargon
- One specific insight about their business
- Soft call-to-action
- Sound like a helpful human, not a salesperson

Variables:
- [PROSPECT_NAME]: From LinkedIn profile
- [PROSPECT_TITLE]: Their current role
- [COMPANY]: Their company name
- [COMPANY_CONTEXT]: Recent news, growth, challenge (research this)
- [YOUR_SERVICE]: What you do in 5 words
- [SPECIFIC_BENEFIT]: Save time/money or make money
```

## Real Example

**Input:**
- [PROSPECT_NAME]: Sarah Chen
- [PROSPECT_TITLE]: VP of Operations
- [COMPANY]: TechFlow Solutions
- [COMPANY_CONTEXT]: Just raised Series B and hiring 50 engineers
- [YOUR_SERVICE]: Developer productivity consulting
- [SPECIFIC_BENEFIT]: Onboard engineers 50% faster

**Output:**
"Hi Sarah, Congrats on the Series B! With 50 engineers joining TechFlow, onboarding efficiency becomes critical. We helped CloudBase cut developer ramp-up from 8 to 4 weeks. Worth a quick call to share what worked?"

**Result:** Meeting booked, $40K contract signed

## Why This Works

1. **Personal research** shows you did homework
2. **Specific benefit** tied to their current challenge
3. **Social proof** with similar company
4. **Soft ask** doesn't feel pushy
5. **Under 300 characters** fits LinkedIn limits

## Success Tips

- Spend 2 minutes researching before writing
- Always tie benefit to current company news/challenges
- Use competitor names for social proof
- A/B test your opening lines
- Follow up 1 week later if no response

## Common Mistakes

❌ "I'd love to connect"
❌ Generic templates
❌ Leading with your pitch
❌ No research about their company
❌ Too long (over 300 characters)
