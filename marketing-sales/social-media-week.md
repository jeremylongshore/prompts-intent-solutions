# Generate Week of Social Media Content (10 minutes)

**Saves:** 4-6 hours/week
**Used by:** 500+ small businesses
**Engagement:** 40% higher than generic posts
**Tested on:** Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook

## The Prompt

```
You are a social media expert for [BUSINESS_TYPE] businesses.

Create 7 days of social media posts for [PLATFORM] that will engage [TARGET_AUDIENCE].

Our business [COMPANY_NAME] helps people [MAIN_VALUE_PROPOSITION].

For each day, provide:
- Post caption (engaging, not salesy)
- 5-8 relevant hashtags
- Best posting time for [TARGET_AUDIENCE]
- Image suggestion (specific, actionable)

Make it authentic and conversational. Mix educational, behind-the-scenes, and customer stories.
No more than 3 promotional posts in the week.

Variables:
- [BUSINESS_TYPE]: bakery, consulting firm, gym, restaurant, etc.
- [PLATFORM]: Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook, TikTok
- [TARGET_AUDIENCE]: busy moms, B2B executives, fitness enthusiasts, etc.
- [COMPANY_NAME]: Your business name
- [MAIN_VALUE_PROPOSITION]: save time, get fit, grow revenue, etc.
```

## Real Example

**Input:**
- [BUSINESS_TYPE]: Local bakery
- [PLATFORM]: Instagram
- [TARGET_AUDIENCE]: Busy parents and coffee lovers
- [COMPANY_NAME]: Sweet Dreams Bakery
- [MAIN_VALUE_PROPOSITION]: Start their day with fresh, homemade treats

**Output:**

### Monday - Behind the Scenes
**Caption:** "4 AM start time means fresh croissants by 7 AM ☕ Our baker Maria has been perfecting her technique for 15 years. What's your morning fuel?"

**Hashtags:** #FreshBaked #BehindTheScenes #LocalBakery #MorningFuel #HandCrafted #SweetDreamsBakery #CoffeeLovers #CroissantLife

**Best Time:** 7:00 AM (commute time)

**Image:** Time-lapse video of croissant making process

### Tuesday - Educational
**Caption:** "Coffee pairing tip: Dark roast ☕ pairs perfectly with our almond croissants, while light roast complements our fruit danish. Which combo will you try tomorrow?"

**Hashtags:** #CoffeePairing #BakingTips #PerfectMatch #AlmondCroissant #FruitDanish #LocalCoffee #TasteTest #SweetDreamsBakery

**Best Time:** 2:00 PM (afternoon coffee break)

**Image:** Split image showing coffee and pastry pairings

### Wednesday - Customer Spotlight
**Caption:** "Sarah brings her twins here every Wednesday after school 👶👶 'It's our special tradition,' she says. We love being part of your family moments! What's your Sweet Dreams tradition?"

**Hashtags:** #CustomerLove #FamilyTradition #RegularCustomers #CommunityLove #WednesdayTreats #KidsChoice #LocalFavorite #SpecialMoments

**Best Time:** 6:00 PM (family dinner time)

**Image:** Photo of Sarah and twins enjoying pastries (with permission)

*[Continues for remaining 4 days...]*

## Why This Works

1. **Mix of content types** keeps followers engaged
2. **Specific posting times** maximize reach
3. **Authentic voice** builds community
4. **Actionable image suggestions** save planning time
5. **Limited promotion** prevents unfollows

## Success Tips

- Batch create on Sunday for the week
- Use scheduling tools (Buffer, Hootsuite)
- Engage with comments within 2 hours
- Repost user-generated content
- Track which posts get most engagement

## Platform Adaptations

### Instagram
- Focus on visual storytelling
- Use Stories for behind-the-scenes
- Include location tags
- Post 1x daily

### LinkedIn
- More professional tone
- Industry insights and tips
- Company culture posts
- Post 3-5x per week

### Facebook
- Longer captions work better
- Community building focus
- Event announcements
- Post 5-7x per week

### TikTok
- Video-first content
- Trends and challenges
- Quick tips format
- Post 3-5x per week
