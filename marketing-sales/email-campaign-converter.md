## The Prompt

```
You are an email marketing expert who writes emails that people actually open and act on.

Create an email campaign for [EMAIL_PURPOSE] to our [AUDIENCE_TYPE].

Email structure:
1. Subject line (curiosity + benefit, under 50 characters)
2. Opening hook (personal story or surprising fact)
3. The problem (what frustrates them daily)
4. The solution (how we solve it specifically)
5. Social proof (customer result with numbers)
6. Clear next step (one action, not multiple options)

Tone: [BRAND_VOICE]
Length: 150-200 words max
Include: Clear CTA button text

Variables:
- [EMAIL_PURPOSE]: Product launch, event invite, content promotion, etc.
- [AUDIENCE_TYPE]: Busy executives, small business owners, freelancers, etc.
- [BRAND_VOICE]: Professional but friendly, casual and fun, expert authority, etc.
```

## Why This Works

1. **Subject line** creates curiosity + promises solution
2. **Personal story** makes it relatable, not corporate
3. **Specific problem** every freelancer recognizes
4. **Clear solution** with concrete details
5. **Social proof** with name and percentage
6. **Single CTA** removes decision paralysis

## Subject Line Formulas

### Curiosity + Benefit
- "Your biggest [PROBLEM] just got fixed"
- "[NUMBER] minute solution to [DAILY_FRUSTRATION]"
- "Why [CUSTOMER_TYPE] are switching to [SOLUTION]"

### Urgency + Value
- "Last chance: [BENEFIT]"
- "[NUMBER] hours left to [GET_BENEFIT]"
- "Ending tonight: [VALUE_PROPOSITION]"

### Personal + Direct
- "[NAME], quick question about [THEIR_CHALLENGE]"
- "Noticed you're struggling with [SPECIFIC_ISSUE]"
- "Your [INDUSTRY] peers are doing this"

## A/B Testing Tips

**Test These Elements:**
- Subject line variations (curiosity vs direct)
- Opening hook (story vs statistic)
- CTA button text ("Try Free" vs "Start Now")
- Email length (short vs medium)
- Send time (Tuesday 10am vs Thursday 2pm)

**Measure These Metrics:**
- Open rate (subject line effectiveness)
- Click rate (content engagement)
- Conversion rate (offer appeal)
- Unsubscribe rate (audience fit)

## Common Mistakes

❌ Multiple CTAs (confuses readers)
❌ Too much company talk ("We're excited to announce...")
❌ Vague benefits ("Increase productivity")
❌ No social proof
❌ Generic subject lines ("Newsletter #47")

## Email Sequence Strategy

### 3-Email Welcome Series
1. **Email 1:** Welcome + Quick win
2. **Email 2:** Main value proposition + social proof
3. **Email 3:** Address common objection + trial/demo

### 5-Email Product Launch
1. **Email 1:** Tease the problem
2. **Email 2:** Reveal the solution
3. **Email 3:** Social proof and testimonials
4. **Email 4:** Limited-time offer
5. **Email 5:** Last chance + FOMO
