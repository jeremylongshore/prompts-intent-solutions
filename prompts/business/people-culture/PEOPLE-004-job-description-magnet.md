### 9. BIAS MITIGATION & INCLUSION

#### Inclusive Language Framework
```markdown
## Language Audit Checklist
### Avoid These Terms
❌ "Rockstar," "Ninja," "Guru" (exclude non-traditional candidates)
❌ "Cultural fit" (bias toward homogeneous hiring)
❌ "Fast-paced" (may deter methodical workers)
❌ "Young team" (age discrimination)
❌ "Native speaker" (language bias)

### Use These Instead
✅ "High-performing," "Expert," "Specialist"
✅ "Values alignment," "Working style match"
✅ "Dynamic," "Collaborative," "Results-oriented"
✅ "Energetic," "Innovative team"
✅ "Strong communication skills"

## Inclusive Requirements
### Skills-Based Focus
- Lead with skills and outcomes, not degrees
- Include "equivalent experience" options
- Focus on learnable vs. innate abilities
- Offer multiple ways to demonstrate qualifications

### Accessibility Considerations
- Include accommodation statement
- Avoid physical requirements unless essential
- Mention flexible work arrangements
- Use clear, simple language structure
```

## EXPERT TIPS FOR MAXIMUM IMPACT

### Psychology-Based Improvements
1. **Start with impact, not company**: Lead with what they'll accomplish
2. **Use specific numbers**: "Manage $2M budget" vs "manage large budget"
3. **Include growth timeline**: "Promotion to Senior level within 18 months"
4. **Address current frustrations**: "No more endless meetings" or "Real work-life balance"
5. **Show peer success**: "Our last hire was promoted in 14 months"

### Platform-Specific Hacks
1. **LinkedIn**: Use hashtags that candidates follow, not recruiter hashtags
2. **Indeed**: Include salary range for 75% more applications
3. **Company site**: Include employee testimonials and culture videos
4. **Glassdoor**: Ensure job description matches company reviews tone

### Conversion Optimization
1. **Multiple calls-to-action**: "Apply now" appears 2-3 times
2. **Urgency without pressure**: "Accepting applications through [date]"
3. **Easy application process**: Remove unnecessary requirements
4. **Mobile optimization**: 60% of candidates apply on mobile

### Quality Filtering Techniques
1. **Specific requirements**: "3+ years with React" vs "JavaScript experience"
2. **Culture questions**: Include 1-2 culture-fit questions in application
3. **Portfolio requirements**: Ask for specific work samples
4. **Values alignment**: Include company values and ask for examples
