### 3. STRUCTURED SCREENING SYSTEM

#### Phone Screen Framework (15-20 minutes)
```markdown
## Pre-Interview Qualification
**Objective**: Verify basic qualifications and mutual interest

### Required Questions (5 minutes)
1. **Availability**: "When could you start if selected?"
2. **Compensation**: "What salary range are you targeting?"
3. **Must-have check**: "Do you have [specific requirement]?"

### Engagement Questions (10 minutes)
1. **Motivation**: "What interests you most about this role?"
2. **Experience**: "Tell me about your experience with [key requirement]"
3. **Situation**: "Describe a situation where you [relevant scenario]"

### Next Steps Decision Matrix
- **Advance to interview**: All requirements met + strong engagement
- **Maybe pile**: Missing 1 requirement but strong potential
- **No**: Missing multiple requirements or poor fit signals
```

#### Structured Interview Framework (60 minutes)
```markdown
## Interview Panel Structure

### Panel Composition
- **Hiring Manager** (30 min): Role-specific competencies
- **Team Member** (15 min): Cultural fit and collaboration
- **Senior Leader** (15 min): Strategic thinking and growth potential

### Core Interview Questions by Competency

#### Problem-Solving (Hiring Manager)
**Question**: "Describe a complex problem you solved recently. Walk me through your approach."
**Evaluation Criteria**:
- Problem identification (1-5)
- Solution methodology (1-5)
- Implementation execution (1-5)
- Results achieved (1-5)

#### Collaboration (Team Member)
**Question**: "Tell me about a time you had to work with a difficult team member."
**Evaluation Criteria**:
- Communication approach (1-5)
- Conflict resolution (1-5)
- Team impact (1-5)
- Learning/growth (1-5)

#### Strategic Thinking (Senior Leader)
**Question**: "Where do you see [relevant field/industry] heading in the next 2-3 years?"
**Evaluation Criteria**:
- Industry knowledge (1-5)
- Future vision (1-5)
- Business acumen (1-5)
- Innovation thinking (1-5)
```

### 5. LEGAL COMPLIANCE CHECKLIST
```markdown
## Hiring Compliance Verification

### Pre-Interview
- [ ] Job description reviewed for discriminatory language
- [ ] Interview questions focus on job-related competencies
- [ ] Accommodation requests addressed appropriately
- [ ] Background check policy communicated clearly

### During Interview
- [ ] No questions about protected characteristics
- [ ] Consistent questions across all candidates
- [ ] Reasonable accommodations provided if requested
- [ ] Professional, job-focused conversation

### Post-Interview
- [ ] Documented job-related reasons for decisions
- [ ] Reference checks conducted consistently
- [ ] Background checks comply with local laws
- [ ] Offer letters include required legal language

### Red Flag Questions to NEVER Ask
❌ Age, marital status, pregnancy plans
❌ Religion, political beliefs, sexual orientation
❌ Disability status (unless directly job-related)
❌ Arrest record (vs. conviction record where legally allowed)
```
