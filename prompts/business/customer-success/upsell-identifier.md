## The Prompt

```
You are an expansion revenue expert who identifies natural upsell opportunities based on customer behavior patterns.

Customer data to analyze:
- Account: [CUSTOMER_NAME]
- Current plan: [CURRENT_PLAN]
- Monthly usage: [USAGE_METRICS]
- Team size: [TEAM_SIZE]
- Industry: [INDUSTRY]
- Account age: [MONTHS_ACTIVE]
- Support tickets: [RECENT_ISSUES]
- Feature requests: [REQUESTED_FEATURES]
- Growth indicators: [GROWTH_SIGNALS]

Identify upsell opportunities by analyzing:

1. USAGE PATTERNS
- Are they hitting plan limits consistently?
- Which features are they using most heavily?
- What usage trends show growth potential?

2. BEHAVIORAL SIGNALS
- Are they trying to access premium features?
- Do support tickets reveal unmet needs?
- Are they adding team members frequently?

3. TIMING INDICATORS
- Is renewal coming up?
- Did they just get funding/budget approval?
- Are they in a growth phase (hiring, expanding)?

4. NATURAL PROGRESSION
- What's the logical next step in their journey?
- Which upgrade would solve their biggest pain?
- What do similar customers typically upgrade to?

Provide:
- Primary upsell recommendation with reasoning
- Timing suggestion (when to approach)
- Messaging strategy (how to position)
- Alternative options (backup recommendations)
- Risk assessment (likelihood of acceptance)

Format as clear action plan with specific next steps.

Variables:
- [CUSTOMER_NAME]: Company/account name
- [CURRENT_PLAN]: Their existing subscription level
- [USAGE_METRICS]: Key usage data points
- [TEAM_SIZE]: Number of active users
- [INDUSTRY]: Business sector/vertical
- [MONTHS_ACTIVE]: How long they've been customers
- [RECENT_ISSUES]: Support tickets or problems
- [REQUESTED_FEATURES]: What they've asked for
- [GROWTH_SIGNALS]: Signs of business expansion
```

## Real Example

**Input:**
- [CUSTOMER_NAME]: TechFlow Solutions
- [CURRENT_PLAN]: Professional ($99/month, 10 users)
- [USAGE_METRICS]: 95% storage used, 18 integrations active, 847 automations/month
- [TEAM_SIZE]: 9 active users (started with 4)
- [INDUSTRY]: Digital marketing agency
- [MONTHS_ACTIVE]: 8 months
- [RECENT_ISSUES]: 3 tickets about hitting automation limits, 1 about storage
- [REQUESTED_FEATURES]: Advanced reporting, white-label options, API access
- [GROWTH_SIGNALS]: Just hired 3 new team members, mentioned new client wins

**Output:**

## UPSELL OPPORTUNITY ANALYSIS: TechFlow Solutions

### 🎯 PRIMARY RECOMMENDATION: Enterprise Plan ($299/month)

**Why this makes sense:**
- Currently at 95% storage capacity (clear pain point)
- Using 847/1000 monthly automations (will hit limit within 30 days)
- Requesting white-label features (indicates client-facing needs)
- Team growth from 4 to 9 users suggests business expansion

**ROI for customer:**
- Unlimited automations = 40+ hours saved monthly
- White-label features = ability to charge clients premium rates
- Advanced reporting = better client retention and upsells

### ⏰ OPTIMAL TIMING: Next 2 weeks

**Trigger signals:**
- They'll hit automation limits before month-end
- New team members need onboarding to avoid plan limits
- Renewal is in 4 months (perfect upgrade timing)

### 💬 MESSAGING STRATEGY

**Opening approach:**
"I noticed TechFlow is crushing it with growth - 9 team members now and hitting those automation limits regularly. That's a great problem to have! I've got some ideas on how we can keep pace with your expansion without any workflow interruptions."

**Value positioning:**
1. **Pain relief**: "No more hitting limits mid-campaign"
2. **Growth enabler**: "White-label features = new revenue stream"
3. **Competitive advantage**: "Advanced reporting your competitors can't match"

**Specific pitch:**
"The Enterprise plan would give you unlimited automations (so you never pause campaigns), white-label options (charge clients premium for 'your' platform), and advanced reporting (prove ROI to retain clients longer). Based on your growth trajectory, this typically pays for itself within the first month."

## Why This Works

1. **Data-driven insights** - Uses actual usage patterns vs. guessing
2. **Pain point focus** - Addresses current frustrations first
3. **Growth alignment** - Connects upgrade to business objectives
4. **Multiple options** - Provides alternatives if primary fails
5. **Timing optimization** - Strikes when need is most acute
6. **Risk assessment** - Sets realistic expectations
