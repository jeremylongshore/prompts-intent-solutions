# How Sweet Dreams Bakery Increased Sales 23% with Social Media Prompts

**Business:** Sweet Dreams Bakery (Local bakery, 2 locations)
**Challenge:** Inconsistent social media, low customer engagement
**Solution:** Weekly social media content generation prompt
**Result:** 23% sales increase in 3 months

## The Challenge

Maria Rodriguez owns Sweet Dreams Bakery with two locations in suburban Phoenix. Despite having loyal customers, she struggled with social media marketing:

- **Time crunch:** Baking from 4 AM left no energy for content creation
- **Inconsistent posting:** Sometimes daily, sometimes not for weeks
- **Generic content:** Photos of pastries with basic captions
- **Low engagement:** 15-20 likes per post, few comments
- **No growth:** Follower count stuck at 800 for 6 months

"I knew social media was important, but I just didn't have time to be creative every day. My posts were boring - just 'fresh croissants today!' over and over."

## Lessons Learned

### What Worked Best
- **Personal stories** performed 3x better than product shots
- **Customer features** generated the most engagement
- **Behind-the-scenes content** built emotional connection
- **Consistent posting times** (7 AM, 2 PM, 6 PM) maximized reach

### Surprising Discoveries
- **Video content** wasn't necessary - well-captioned photos worked great
- **Hashtags mattered less** than genuine storytelling
- **Local references** (Phoenix heat, Arizona Cardinals games) boosted engagement
- **Questions in captions** consistently doubled comment rates

### Common Mistakes Avoided
- Not over-promoting products (stuck to 80/20 rule: 80% story, 20% sales)
- Avoiding corporate language ("We're excited to announce...")
- Not using stock photography (customers wanted real bakery photos)
- Engaging authentically in comments (not just liking them)

## Implementation Tips for Other Bakeries

### Getting Started
1. **Batch content creation:** One session per week, not daily scrambling
2. **Photo planning:** Take multiple angles during slow periods
3. **Voice development:** Write like you're talking to a neighbor
4. **Community focus:** Feature customers, not just products

### Content Calendar Adaptation
- **Monday:** Weekend recap + week ahead
- **Tuesday:** Ingredient spotlights (local suppliers)
- **Wednesday:** Customer features
- **Thursday:** Technique/educational posts
- **Friday:** Weekend specials preview
- **Saturday:** Community events/partnerships
- **Sunday:** Sunday funday/family content

### Measuring Success
- Track engagement rates, not just follower count
- Monitor foot traffic patterns after posts
- Ask new customers how they found you
- Measure special order requests from social media
